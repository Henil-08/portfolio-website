My journey into ML started in an unexpected place, I was preparing an autonomous car for an electric vehicle competition during my undergrad in Electronics and Communication Engineering. As team captain, I dove headfirst into computer vision and machine learning, and honestly, I was hooked. That curiosity led me to join Hyperlab as a Founding ML Engineer, where we built an ML-driven sports analytics app that got us featured on Shark Tank India - easily one of the proudest moments of my life. But more than the spotlight, Hyperlab taught me how to ship products people actually use, close deals, optimize under constraints, and build fast.

I moved to the US to deepen my expertise in Data Science at Northeastern, and the GenAI boom happened. Since then, I've been laser-focused on agentic AI, RAG, LLM fine-tuning, and production systems. Through three co-ops at Waters Corporation, Edlight, and ImpacterAI, I've built chatbots, optimized inference, and automated LLM evaluations. I've learned that great ML engineering isn't just about knowing the frameworks - it's about making smart tradeoffs, asking hard questions, and building systems that scale under real-world pressure.

Now wrapping up my MS in Data Science (4.0 GPA, Dec 2025), I'm actively seeking full-time ML Engineer roles starting January 2026 where I can continue building production AI that makes an impact.
